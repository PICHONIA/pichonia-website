Transform `pichonia.com` from a standard starter template into an elite, intelligent, high-conversion technical flagship that visually represents an institutional-grade engineering studio and product incubator.

* **Dynamic Hero Section:** Replace generic placeholder copy with a bold value proposition highlighting dual-track engineering (Proprietary AI/Logistics incubation + Bespoke B2B systems).
* **The Studio Portfolio Module:** Dedicated interactive cards showcasing internal ventures:
  * *Pichonia Logistics Engine ("Uber for Trucks" & White-Label Fleet SaaS)*
  * *Derbest Options Trading AI (Predictive models & automated execution)*
* **Build-In-Public Engineering Log:** A dedicated `/blog` or `/engineering` route powered by markdown files, automatically publishing our infrastructure post-mortems and CI/CD case studies.
* **Refined Typography & Dark-Mode Aesthetics:** Clean, high-contrast typography, subtle grid borders, and technical UI elements that signal elite software engineering.
